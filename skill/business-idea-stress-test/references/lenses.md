# Business idea weakness lenses

Apply only lenses capable of changing the decision. Record a lens as “not applicable” only when the reason matters; otherwise omit it.

1. **Decision and owner** — Who can approve, reject, fund, or stop this?
2. **Problem and beneficiary** — Whose problem is this, and how do they experience it today?
3. **Measured baseline** — What volume, time, cost, error, delay, or dissatisfaction exists now?
4. **Do nothing** — What happens if nothing changes, including costs avoided?
5. **Alternatives** — What cheaper, smaller, manual, internal, or existing option competes with this?
6. **Adoption** — Who must change behaviour, and why would they?
7. **Who loses** — Who loses control, budget, status, convenience, or headcount?
8. **Value location** — Whose budget pays and whose budget receives the benefit?
9. **Full cost** — Include implementation, integration, internal effort, training, support, renewal, exit, and stopping.
10. **Data and governance** — What data, security, privacy, legal, regulatory, or works-council review applies?
11. **Operational ownership** — Who owns the result after the launch team leaves?
12. **Failure tolerance** — What happens when the process, vendor, automation, or output is wrong?
13. **Dependencies and lead times** — Which systems, contracts, vendors, teams, or approvals can set the real deadline?
14. **Reversibility and lock-in** — Can the organisation stop or switch, and at what cost?
15. **Measurement** — Which single measure would show that the idea worked, who owns it, and when is it read?
16. **Kill criteria** — Which result should stop the pilot or investment, and when is that decision made?
17. **Day 91** — After the launch attention ends, who operates, supports, funds, measures, and improves it?

For each load-bearing weakness, capture the cheapest test that could disprove the assumption before significant commitment.
