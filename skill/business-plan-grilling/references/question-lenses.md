# Business plan question lenses

Choose only the sections matching the plan and only questions whose answers are not already settled.

## Core

- Who receives the output or change, and what decision or behaviour should follow?
- What exactly is being approved, funded, changed, or delivered?
- What measurable result should exist after three months?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- Which deadline, budget, format, dependency, or approval constrains the work?
- Has this been attempted before, and what was learned?
- Who is likely to resist, and why?
- What data or governance review applies?

## Money

- Is the baseline measured or estimated?
- Does the cost include implementation, integration, training, support, renewal, and exit?
- Whose budget pays, whose budget benefits, and who sponsors the mismatch?
- What cheaper version captures most of the value?

## Process or organisation change

- Who must behave differently, and what is in it for them?
- Who loses control, budget, status, convenience, or headcount?
- What rollback is possible and what does it cost?
- Who owns communication, training, documentation, and support?

## Tool or vendor

- What alternatives are being compared, including doing nothing and using an existing tool?
- Where is the lock-in and what does leaving cost?
- Where is the data stored and who processes it?
- Who owns the vendor relationship and renewal after go-live?

## AI or automation

- Is the underlying process stable enough to automate?
- What volume and current cost per case are evidenced?
- What is the tolerance for a wrong output and where is human review required?
- Who owns performance, incidents, drift, and improvement after the pilot?
- Which use-case, privacy, security, legal, or works-council approval is needed before work starts?

## Document, communication, or presentation

- Who is in the room and what do they already believe?
- What single sentence must they remember?
- What decision should they make?
- What is the hardest likely question, and what evidence supports the answer?
