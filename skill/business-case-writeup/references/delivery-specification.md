# Delivery specification reference

Adapt this structure to the work. Requirements and acceptance criteria must be testable by someone who was not in the conversation.

```markdown
# <Specific delivery title>

**Owner:** <accountable person or TBD> | **Status:** Draft | **Date:** <date>
**Approved by / based on:** <decision or TBD>

## Purpose

<What this delivers and why.>

## Scope

### In scope

- <Item>

### Out of scope

- <Item>

## Who is affected

| Role, team, location, or system | Effect |
| ------------------------------- | ------ |

## Requirements

### Functional

1. The result must <testable behaviour>.

### Non-functional

1. The result must <testable volume, performance, availability, language, accessibility, retention, or auditability requirement>.

## Process and responsibilities

<Who does what, in what order, who approves, and what happens on an exception. Use a RACI table only when responsibility is genuinely contested.>

## Decisions taken

| Decision | Reason | Rejected alternative |
| -------- | ------ | -------------------- |

## Dependencies

| Dependency | Owner | Needed by | What it blocks |
| ---------- | ----- | --------- | -------------- |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner |
| ---- | ------ | ---------- | ---------- | ----- |

## Data and governance

<Data categories, personal-data scope, system of record, retention, and applicable governance, security, legal, or works-council steps. Write “None identified” only when supported.>

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] <Verifiable result>

## Open questions and assumptions

| Type | Statement | Confirmation owner | What it blocks |
| ---- | --------- | ------------------ | -------------- |
```
